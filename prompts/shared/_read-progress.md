## Progress while you read

This mode changes nothing, but silence is still expensive: a tool call is
invisible in the transcript, and a long quiet stretch reads as a hang. Every 20
tool calls — or 10 minutes, whichever comes first — print 2–3 lines in the
user's language: what you have established, what you are checking now, and what
is still open.

Mark interim findings as interim. Keep the final answer for the end, where every
claim cites the path, command, or citation behind it.
